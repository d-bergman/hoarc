What to expect

A welcoming community

Whether this is your first church visit or you are looking for a new place to worship, we want you to feel welcome.

Take your time

Services usually last 1½–2 hours, with an opportunity for personal prayer at the close.

Come as you are

Casual to casual-formal clothing is welcome. Modest dress is requested.

Bring your family

Children are welcome. Regular childcare and children’s programs are planned for the future.

Arriving on Sunday

Allow a little extra time to park and find the first-floor entrance. Contact us for help with your visit.

Accessibility: Our pastor reports the building is wheelchair accessible. Please contact us about entrance or accessibility assistance while the specific route and room details are being confirmed.

Prayer meetings, Bible studies, and additional gatherings are planned for 2027. Stay in touch for details.
