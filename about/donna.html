Our people

Donna DeSantis

Donna DeSantis serves with a deep commitment to prayer, ministry, and helping others experience the love of Jesus Christ. She has a particular heart for intercession and faithfully dedicates time to praying for individuals, families, and the needs of the Church, often praying throughout the night as she feels led by God.

Donna is passionate about reaching those who do not yet know Christ and encouraging believers who are navigating difficult seasons of life. Her compassion for the lost and the hurting is reflected in her willingness to pray with others, share the Gospel, and offer spiritual encouragement.

She has been actively involved in ministry since 2002 and also serves in ministry administration.

Donna has studied Biblical Studies and earned a Diploma in Ministerial Training through Aquila Christian International. She is a licensed minister with the Association of International Gospel Assemblies (A.I.G.A.).

Above all, Donna desires to remain faithful to God’s calling, stand in prayer for others, and help people discover the peace, hope, and love found in Jesus Christ.
