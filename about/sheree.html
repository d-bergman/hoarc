Our people

Sherée Bergman

Sherée Bergman is a Certified Chaplain and experienced coach who has dedicated many years to helping individuals navigate life’s challenges, overcome emotional obstacles, and discover renewed hope and purpose.

Since 2007, she has served individuals through coaching, counseling, and ministry, combining professional training, personal experience, and a strong dependence on God’s wisdom.

Sherée has a particular passion for helping people address emotional wounds rooted in childhood experiences. She recognizes that unresolved hurts from the past can influence relationships, choices, and emotional well-being in the present.

Through a compassionate and personalized approach, she encourages individuals to recognize unhealthy patterns, develop greater self-awareness, and pursue emotional restoration and personal growth.

As an Empowerment Life Coach, Sherée helps people identify and work through what she calls their “Glass Walls” — the unseen barriers that can keep individuals from moving forward into the life and purpose God has for them.

Her desire is to see people experience emotional freedom, rediscover their God-given worth, and move forward with greater confidence, faith, and hope.
