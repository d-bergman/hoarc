Our people

Darren Bergman

Together with his wife, Bhumika, Darren Bergman shares a heart for serving God, helping those in need, and encouraging people through their personal journeys of faith. Both have experienced challenges that have strengthened their compassion for others and deepened their desire to share the hope found in Jesus Christ.

Darren’s own testimony is one of hardship, perseverance, and discovering God’s faithfulness through difficult seasons of life. His experiences have given him a particular compassion for individuals who feel misunderstood, overlooked, or without hope. He believes that no one is beyond the reach of God’s love and that even the most difficult experiences can become opportunities to encourage and uplift others.

Darren also has a heart for young people and enjoys connecting with them, offering encouragement, and helping them recognize their value and potential. He values genuine relationships and believes that kindness, patience, and understanding can make a meaningful difference in someone’s life.

Music is an important part of Darren’s relationship with God. As a guitarist and songwriter, he enjoys worshiping the Lord through music and creating original Christian songs with Bhumika. Together, they established Polaris Frequency Studio, an online music and creative outreach ministry dedicated to sharing God’s message through Scripture-inspired worship, personal testimonies, and music centered on faith, hope, and restoration.

Their ministry seeks to reach people wherever they are in their spiritual journey — whether they are searching for God, struggling with their faith, returning to Him, or seeking a deeper relationship with Christ. Through music, they hope to encourage listeners and draw hearts closer to God.

With a love for technology and creative expression, Darren also contributes his technical and artistic abilities to church media, communications, and outreach efforts.

Above all, Darren desires to honor God through his life, encourage others through his testimony, and use his gifts to share the transforming love and grace of Jesus Christ.
