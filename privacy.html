Privacy Policy

We respect the information you choose to share with us.

Getting in touch

When you email or call the church, we use the information you provide to respond to your inquiry. If the website contact form is available, your name, email address, optional telephone number, subject, and message are sent privately to the church inbox. Messages are not published on this website.

Please avoid sending sensitive personal information. Contact messages are reviewed by authorized church personnel and are not a confidential counseling service.

Website services

The site uses Firebase for hosting and published announcements. Administrators use Firebase Authentication to manage notices. The site does not use advertising trackers or analytics. Operational services may process technical information such as IP addresses for security, abuse prevention, and delivery.

Where the contact form is enabled, temporary rate-limit records contain a keyed hash of an IP address and a request count, rather than message content. Expired records are removed by scheduled cleanup.

Links to other services

Google Maps, Bible Gateway, Amazon, PayPal, YouTube, Facebook, and Instagram links take you to external services with their own privacy practices. The homepage requests the daily Scripture from Bible Gateway. Maps, social feeds, and music are not automatically embedded. Fonts are served locally.

Questions and requests

Contact hoarc.church@gmail.com to ask about information you have shared or request its removal. The church will review requests and any applicable recordkeeping requirements.
