What We Believe

Healing of America Revival Center is a division of Aquila Christian Ministries.

Our desire is to disciple and equip believers for their God-appointed purpose, encourage revival that reaches beyond our walls, and minister healing and restoration to spirit, soul, and body.

As a non-denominational Christian ministry, our beliefs are grounded in the Bible and shape our worship, teaching, prayer, and service.

Senior Pastor Larry DeSantis

Larry DeSantis is the founder of Aquila Christian Ministries and Healing of America Revival Center. He received Jesus Christ as his Lord and Savior on November 18, 1980, and has faithfully preached the Gospel since 1986.

Larry has a deep love for God’s people and a passion for reaching those who have yet to know Christ. His ministry emphasizes the full Gospel, intimacy with God, the love of the Father, and the call for spiritual revival.

Throughout his years in ministry, Larry has received prophetic words concerning revival, including from Evangelist Hector De La Cruz of Mexico. He believes the Church stands at the threshold of a significant spiritual awakening, marked by deep repentance, renewed devotion to God, and a powerful outpouring of the Father’s love.

His desire is to see people set free from spiritual bondage, believers restored to a deeper relationship with Christ, and the Church in America return wholeheartedly to God. He believes this is a time for spiritual renewal and for the healing of America through the transforming power of Jesus Christ.

In addition to preaching and ministry leadership, Larry teaches and consults on spiritual and natural health, seeking to help individuals understand the importance of caring for both spiritual and physical well-being.

Education, credentials and ministry experience

Donna DeSantis

Donna DeSantis serves with a deep commitment to prayer, ministry, and helping others experience the love of Jesus Christ. She has a particular heart for intercession and faithfully dedicates time to praying for individuals, families, and the needs of the Church, often praying throughout the night as she feels led by God.

Donna is passionate about reaching those who do not yet know Christ and encouraging believers who are navigating difficult seasons of life. Her compassion for the lost and the hurting is reflected in her willingness to pray with others, share the Gospel, and offer spiritual encouragement.

She has been actively involved in ministry since 2002 and also serves in ministry administration.

Donna has studied Biblical Studies and earned a Diploma in Ministerial Training through Aquila Christian International. She is a licensed minister with the Association of International Gospel Assemblies (A.I.G.A.).

Above all, Donna desires to remain faithful to God’s calling, stand in prayer for others, and help people discover the peace, hope, and love found in Jesus Christ.

Sherée Bergman

Sherée Bergman is a Certified Chaplain and experienced coach who has dedicated many years to helping individuals navigate life’s challenges, overcome emotional obstacles, and discover renewed hope and purpose.

Since 2007, she has served individuals through coaching, counseling, and ministry, combining professional training, personal experience, and a strong dependence on God’s wisdom.

Sherée has a particular passion for helping people address emotional wounds rooted in childhood experiences. She recognizes that unresolved hurts from the past can influence relationships, choices, and emotional well-being in the present.

Through a compassionate and personalized approach, she encourages individuals to recognize unhealthy patterns, develop greater self-awareness, and pursue emotional restoration and personal growth.

As an Empowerment Life Coach, Sherée helps people identify and work through what she calls their “Glass Walls” — the unseen barriers that can keep individuals from moving forward into the life and purpose God has for them.

Her desire is to see people experience emotional freedom, rediscover their God-given worth, and move forward with greater confidence, faith, and hope.

Darren Bergman

Together with his wife, Bhumika, Darren Bergman shares a heart for serving God, helping those in need, and encouraging people through their personal journeys of faith. Both have experienced challenges that have strengthened their compassion for others and deepened their desire to share the hope found in Jesus Christ.

Darren’s own testimony is one of hardship, perseverance, and discovering God’s faithfulness through difficult seasons of life. His experiences have given him a particular compassion for individuals who feel misunderstood, overlooked, or without hope. He believes that no one is beyond the reach of God’s love and that even the most difficult experiences can become opportunities to encourage and uplift others.

Darren also has a heart for young people and enjoys connecting with them, offering encouragement, and helping them recognize their value and potential. He values genuine relationships and believes that kindness, patience, and understanding can make a meaningful difference in someone’s life.

Music is an important part of Darren’s relationship with God. As a guitarist and songwriter, he enjoys worshiping the Lord through music and creating original Christian songs with Bhumika. Together, they established Polaris Frequency Studio, an online music and creative outreach ministry dedicated to sharing God’s message through Scripture-inspired worship, personal testimonies, and music centered on faith, hope, and restoration.

Their ministry seeks to reach people wherever they are in their spiritual journey — whether they are searching for God, struggling with their faith, returning to Him, or seeking a deeper relationship with Christ. Through music, they hope to encourage listeners and draw hearts closer to God.

With a love for technology and creative expression, Darren also contributes his technical and artistic abilities to church media, communications, and outreach efforts.

Above all, Darren desires to honor God through his life, encourage others through his testimony, and use his gifts to share the transforming love and grace of Jesus Christ.

Bhumika Bergman

Together with her husband, Darren, Bhumika Bergman shares a deep love for God and a desire to bring hope, compassion, and encouragement to those who are hurting. Their shared journey of faith has strengthened their commitment to serving others and using their experiences and gifts to glorify God.

Coming from an Indian background, Bhumika’s personal journey of faith has played a significant role in shaping her relationship with Jesus Christ. Through seasons of personal challenges, spiritual growth, and learning to surrender to God’s will, she has developed a deeper understanding of perseverance, grace, and the importance of trusting the Lord.

Bhumika has a particular heart for individuals who have experienced emotional pain and abuse. She serves as a co-leader of the Heart to Heart Hope and Healing support group, which supports women who have experienced emotional abuse. Through this work, she seeks to provide compassion, understanding, and encouragement to women as they navigate their journeys toward emotional healing and restoration.

Her compassion extends to both people and animals, especially those who are suffering, vulnerable, or unable to advocate for themselves. She believes that following Christ means demonstrating His love through kindness, humility, and meaningful acts of service.

Alongside Darren, Bhumika co-created Polaris Frequency Studio, an online music and creative outreach ministry. Through original Christian music inspired by Scripture, worship, and personal testimonies, they seek to encourage people at every stage of their relationship with God. Their desire is to reach those seeking Christ, strengthen those walking through spiritual struggles, and offer hope to those who feel distant from Him.

With a professional background in web development, digital accessibility, design, and communications, Bhumika also enjoys using her creative and technical abilities to support church communications, digital outreach, and marketing.

Above all, Bhumika desires to follow Christ wholeheartedly, show compassion to those in need, and use the gifts and experiences God has given her to make a meaningful difference in the lives of others.
