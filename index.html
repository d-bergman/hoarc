Our mission

Disciple ◆ Equip ◆ Revive ◆ Restore

To disciple and equip believers for their God-appointed purpose, experience and spread revival, and minister healing and restoration to spirit, soul, and body.

We seek to encourage spiritual growth, natural health and wellness, and a life of faith expressed through love for others.
